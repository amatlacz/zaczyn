# Brief — Symposium talk

> The contract for this work item: what "done" looks like, agreed **before** the work starts.
> Created by zaczyn-start-session on 2026-09-01.

## Original request

> "My symposium talk is in five weeks and I haven't started. I have the results, I have no slides. Can we at least get the outline done today?"

## Deliverable

A 20-minute talk in three files: the outline (`content/outline.md`), timed speaker notes (`content/speaker-notes.md`), and the slide deck in the institute template (`exports/talk.pptx`).

## Audience & bar

About forty peers and the three-person symposium committee. "Good enough" in Marta's words: *"a talk I'd be happy to have on the department YouTube channel"* — one clear message, no result without its figure, finishes on time.

## Success criteria

> Numbered and checkable — a reviewer should be able to verify each without asking the author.

- **SC-1:** A timed read-through of the speaker notes takes **≤ 19 minutes** (20-minute slot, one-minute buffer).
- **SC-2:** One take-home message, in one sentence, appears **verbatim** on the first content slide and on the last slide.
- **SC-3:** Every result slide names the figure it shows, and that figure exists in `sources/analysis/`.
- **SC-4:** A "Limitations & next steps" slide is present — the committee asked every speaker for one.
- **SC-5:** The deck uses the institute template (logo, fonts) on every slide.

## Sources & constraints

`sources/analysis/` — figures 1–6 and the summary statistics from the analysis notebook · `sources/template.pptx` — the institute template · slot 8 October 2026, 20 min + 5 Q&A, confirmed in the programme · no new analysis: the results are final.

## Risks

Falsification question — *what would have to be true for this to be the wrong thing to do right now?* — if the committee had changed the slot length or format. Marta checked the programme: 20 minutes, confirmed. The real risk is scope creep: the talk turning into a defence rehearsal with every robustness check on a slide.
